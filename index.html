CQS validation and analytics pipeline

CQS transforms trade-by-trade evidence into normalized, reconciled, auditable and cost-adjusted results before any strategy enters the Official Core.

  1. 01Trade-by-Trade SourcePlatform export, operation by operation, with a recorded SHA-256.
  2. 02Native NormalizationNormalizer v0.5 types, validates and computes the native result.
  3. 03Internal ReconciliationChecks the computed result against the ledger itself. Divergence blocks.
  4. 04Strategy ManifestDeclared contract: asset, exchange, PointValue and quantity.
  5. 05Costs & SlippageNATIVE, S0, S1 and S2 scenarios over official fees and modeled slippage.
  6. 06AnalyticsEquity curve, drawdown, monthly, risk and comparison.
  7. 07Official CQS DecisionHuman GO / NO-GO. The tool never homologates on its own.

Seven stages. No strategy enters the official core without clearing all of them.
